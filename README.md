# World News — 24 November 2026

One image that sums up the world's news of Tuesday 24 November 2026.
Entry for make.fun competition `news-image-20261124`, built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Sum up 24 November's world news in one image" bounty, $100 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

This copies `entry.png` to `out/entry.png` (in the grader it goes to
`/output/entry.png`). Node 22 standard library only, no network.

## How the image was made

`entry.png` is a drawn infographic, not an AI-generated photo. It was
built as HTML/CSS in `infographic.html` and rendered to a 1024x1024 PNG
with headless Chromium. All text is real text from the page, so there
are no garbled words.

## The six stories in the image

1. **Strait of Hormuz — Iran says four tankers hit mines.**
   Iran reported four tankers struck by mines in the Strait of Hormuz.
   The Revolutionary Guard said its navy targeted a container ship it
   accused of switching off its navigation systems and using an
   unauthorised route.
2. **Saudi Arabia — Houthi strike hits Riyadh tower, injuring 11.**
   A Houthi attack on a tower in Riyadh's King Abdullah Financial
   District wounded at least 11 people, the second hit on the building
   in under a week.
3. **United States — Huawei found guilty in US trial.** A federal jury
   convicted Huawei of conspiring to steal trade secrets and bank fraud,
   on 10 of 12 counts, but acquitted it of racketeering.
4. **Ukraine — Ukraine hits Russian ships at sea.** Ukraine said drones
   struck the Russian cargo ship Sparta II in the Gulf of Finland, and
   naval drones hit a Russian corvette and a cargo ship off Libya.
5. **Türkiye — Air Force helicopter crash kills five.** A Turkish Air
   Force AS-532 helicopter crashed shortly after taking off from
   Eskisehir for training, killing all five crew members aboard.
6. **Markets — Dow tops 50,000 as tech slides.** The Dow closed above
   50,000 for the first time, at 50,018.70 (+0.85%), while the Nasdaq
   fell 1.48% to 23,733.54 and Nvidia dropped 5.9% in an AI sell-off.
