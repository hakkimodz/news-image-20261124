STRAIT OF HORMUZ

Iran says four tankers hit mines in the Strait of Hormuz

Iran reported four tankers struck by mines in the strait. Its Revolutionary Guard said its navy targeted a container ship it accused of switching off its navigation systems and using an unauthorised route.

SAUDI ARABIA

Houthi strike hits Riyadh tower, injuring 11

A Houthi attack on a tower in Riyadh’s financial district wounded at least 11 people — the second hit on the building in under a week.

UNITED STATES

Huawei found guilty in US trial

A federal jury convicted Huawei of conspiring to steal trade secrets and bank fraud, on 10 of 12 counts, but acquitted it of racketeering.

UKRAINE

Ukraine hits Russian ships at sea

Ukraine said drones struck the Russian cargo ship Sparta II in the Gulf of Finland, and naval drones hit a Russian corvette and a cargo ship off Libya.

TÜRKIYE

Air Force helicopter crash kills five

A Turkish Air Force AS-532 helicopter crashed shortly after taking off from Eskisehir for training, killing all five crew members aboard.

MARKETS

Dow tops 50,000 as tech slides

The Dow closed above 50,000 for the first time, at 50,018.70, while the Nasdaq fell 1.5% and Nvidia dropped 5.9% in an AI sell-off.
